## Exact Style Rules

- Match the mockup.
- Dark green-black stone.
- Aged brass bevels.
- Muted parchment text.
- Green glow only for active/protected states.
- Carved ornamental corners.
- Subtle grain and stone texture.
- No modern flat SaaS look.
- No new composition.
